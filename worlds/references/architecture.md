# Valenreach architecture brief

Valenreach grew where three trade roads meet the Lume River. River stone carries the lower walls; warm lime plaster and dark timber make the dense town readable against green uplands. Roof color identifies districts: red tile for the old ward, oxidized copper for civic buildings, and canvas for the caravan quarter. Doorways remain clear even when trade clutter accumulates along street-facing walls.

Houses should feel assembled from one local kit without becoming identical. Gables, towers, courtyards, stepped foundations, and material changes provide deliberate variants. Interiors use the same dimensions as their exterior entrances but occupy independent coordinate spaces.
